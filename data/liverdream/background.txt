The LiverDREAM / CellNOpt benchmark is a public HepG2 liver-cell signalling dataset derived from Saez-Rodriguez et al. and distributed with CellNOptR. It measures phosphoprotein responses under cytokine and growth-factor stimulation and kinase inhibition. The measured readouts used here are AKT, MEK1/2, ERK1/2, IkB, JNK1/2, p38, and HSP27, spanning PI3K/AKT, RAF/MEK/ERK, stress MAPK, NF-kB, and heat-shock response signalling.
