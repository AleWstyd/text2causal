The Sachs et al. (2005) dataset measures intracellular signaling activity in primary human naive CD4+ T cells under nine stimulation and inhibition conditions. Each cell is fixed, permeabilised, and stained with a panel of phospho-specific antibodies plus a fluorescent dye for two phosphoinositide lipids; phosphorylation levels for eleven analytes are recorded by multiparameter intracellular flow cytometry. The readouts are phosphorylated RAF1 (column "praf"), MEK1/2 ("pmek"), ERK1/ERK2 ("p44/42"), Akt at serine-473 ("pakts473"), the catalytic subunit of protein kinase A ("PKA"), protein kinase C in a pan-isoform readout ("PKC"), p38 MAPK ("P38"), c-Jun N-terminal kinase ("pjnk"), phospholipase-C gamma ("plcg"), and the phosphoinositide lipids phosphatidylinositol 4,5-bisphosphate ("PIP2") and phosphatidylinositol 3,4,5-trisphosphate ("PIP3").

Textbook biology of the canonical signal-transduction modules these analytes span. In the mitogen-activated protein kinase cascade, signal flows from RAF1 through MEK1/2 to ERK1/ERK2 by sequential phosphorylation. In the phosphoinositide-3-kinase / Akt branch, PI3K phosphorylates PIP2 to PIP3 at the membrane, and PIP3 then recruits Akt to the membrane and primes its phosphorylation at serine-473. In the phospholipase-C arm, phospholipase-C gamma is itself activated by upstream tyrosine kinases and hydrolyses PIP2 to diacylglycerol and inositol-1,4,5-trisphosphate; diacylglycerol activates conventional and novel PKC isoforms. Protein kinase A is regulated by cyclic AMP signaling and acts on multiple downstream kinases including the MAPK cascade. The dataset is a standard benchmark in the bnlearn and Causal Discovery Toolbox ecosystems.
